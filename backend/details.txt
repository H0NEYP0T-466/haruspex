========================================================================
  HARUSPEX — Full Experiment Details
  (Model Performance + Dataset Sizes)
========================================================================

Two phases, two directions:
  Phase 1: Train on BODMAS  -> Test on BODMAS -> Cross-dataset on EMBER 2018
  Phase 2: Train on EMBER   -> Test on EMBER  -> Cross-dataset on BODMAS

Feature space is identical in both directions: 2381 EMBER-compatible
features per sample.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PHASE 1 — MODELS TRAINED ON BODMAS
   Models: outputs/models/xgboost.json, outputs/models/lightgbm.json
   Script: scripts/train.py (+ scripts/evaluate.py)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

TRAINING DATA: BODMAS (backend/dataset/BODMAS/bodmas.npz, 261 MB)
  Full dataset .............. 134,435 samples x 2,381 features
  Class balance ............. 77,142 benign / 57,293 malware (42.6% malware)
  Malware families .......... 582
  Metadata .................. bodmas_metadata.csv (sha, timestamp, family)
                              bodmas_malware_category.csv (14 categories)

  Random split (80/20 stratified, seed=42) — the split models are trained on:
    Train ................... 107,548 samples (42.6% malware)
      -> internal validation carve-out (10% of train, trainer.py):
         10,755 val / 96,793 actually fitted
    Test .................... 26,887 samples (42.6% malware)

OTHER EVALUATION SPLITS ON BODMAS (same trained models, different partitions):
  Family-held-out (zero-day by family):
    Held-out families ....... gepys, benjamin, musecador, autoit
    Train ................... 130,217 samples (40.8% malware)
    Test .................... 4,218 samples (100% malware)
  Temporal (zero-day by time), cutoff = 2020-06-01:
    Train ................... 95,537 samples (41.5% malware)
    Test .................... 38,898 samples (45.3% malware)

5. KEY OBSERVATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - BODMAS-trained models drop from ~99.7% to ~84% on EMBER 2018
    (cross-domain generalization gap).
  - EMBER-trained models hold up much better on BODMAS (~96%), likely due
    to training on 4x more data (432K vs 97K fitted samples).
  - The 10K vs 200K EMBER subsample barely changes cross-dataset accuracy
    (XGB: 0.8390 vs 0.8417) — the 10K default subsample is representative.
  - Sources: training_logs_p2.txt, testing_ember.txt,
    outputs/reports/evaluation_results_*.json,
    outputs/reports/phase2_training_summary.json
